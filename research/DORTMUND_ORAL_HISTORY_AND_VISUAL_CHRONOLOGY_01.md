## 1. ATOM ONE — ILOVEGRAFFITI Podcast #040 (7 December 2020)

**Source:** https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/2020/12/07/podcast-040-atom-one-ueber-graffiti-in-dortmund-new-york-too-strong-the-real-deal/

The episode page identifies ATOM ONE (also DER LANGE) and describes the interview as covering:
- his early and later graffiti years;
- the internationally known blocky Dortmund style;
- his first trip to New York;
- Amsterdam influences;
- visits from Berlin;
- generational conflicts;
- rap, TOO STRONG and the local cultural context.

The publisher says the conversation was recorded for *The Rise of Graffiti Writing — From New York to Europe* and that parts appear in Season 3, Episodes 01 and 05.

The same page identifies TOO STRONG as a formation founded in 1989 by DJ Zonic and Pure Doze, with DJ Brocke and DER LANGE joining shortly afterwards. This is a useful cross-domain source, but it does not make ATOM ONE / DER LANGE the same person as Pure Doze; preserve those identities distinctly.

**Evidence boundary:** the article's editorial summary verifies the interview's subject areas, not the exact statements made in the audio. No timecoded transcript has been produced here. Treat each historical claim as unverified until the episode is listened to and attributed.

## 2. TWISTER / JEZUS / PRIDE — ILOVEGRAFFITI Podcast #059 (20 October 2021)

**Source:** https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/2021/10/20/podcast-059-smalltalk-mit-twister-a-k-a-jezus-aus-dortmund/

The publisher identifies the guest as TWISTER, also known as JEZUS and PRIDE, and describes an approximately one-hour conversation about:
- graffiti in the Ruhrgebiet;
- wholecars;
- blocky letters;
- fat caps;
- the writer's return to train panels from 2018;
- stylistic influence from Switzerland and northern Germany.

The post notes that the recording's audio quality declines near the end and that the guest's voice is deliberately distorted for investigative reasons. This limitation should be recorded in any future transcription.

**Value:** a later-generation / return-to-trainwriting perspective. It can help test how a writer from the older Dortmund school understands continuity and change, but it is not direct evidence of the exact 1980s form unless the speaker explicitly recalls and dates it.

## 3. *IRON CITY — Graffiti Dortmund* (MASmedia, 2002)

**Source records:**
- Hitzerot publication description: https://hitzerot.com/iron-city/
- MASON's shop listing: https://shop.mason.de/product/iron-city-graffiti-dortmund-2002-signed

The Hitzerot description records:
- 128 pages;
- German and English;
- release in 2002;
- publisher MASmedia;
- rare photographs of early writing beginning in 1983;
- full-colour panels from 1986;
- photographic documentation of Dortmund's completely painted railway system in the 1990s;
- a period-organized selection covering the 1980s (ROC, COLE, ZHIT), 1990s (IZM, RIO, SKE3, DNK, MASON) and early 2000s (WUT, PORE, REVO, TAPS).

MASON's own shop listing describes the book as a 1983–2002 visual development of illegal piecing on trains and walls. These are publisher / bookseller descriptions, not independent page-level verification.

**Value:** this is the strongest identified single book-level source route for a visual chronology spanning early Dortmund activity, mid-1980s colour panels, the 1990s train system and early-2000s writing. It potentially bridges the gaps between *Da kukse wa* (1987), *Graffiti Dortmund* (1996) and *ALL SURFACE* (2022).

**Rights:** no page images, scans or derivative crops have been copied. Publication purchase, listing or public preview does not grant reuse rights.

2. Pair the book's item-level captions with *Da kukse wa* and *ALL SURFACE*; do not substitute retrospective marketing copy for direct visual inspection.

5. Keep TUF, TOO STRONG, individual writers and the wider Dortmund hip-hop scene as distinct entities. A shared interview or music project is not proof of graffiti crew membership.

7. Keep all visual material reference-only until the source page, creator, caption, date and rights status are checked.
