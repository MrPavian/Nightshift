**Date:** 2026-11-14

**Purpose:** recover a practical first-person source route cited by Harald Hinz and strengthen the Dortmund interview archive without inventing testimony.

## 1. Two accessible documentary episodes

### A. *The Rise of Graffiti Writing — Dortmund, Berlin & Frankfurt*
- Series: *The Rise of Graffiti Writing — From New York to Europe*
- Season 3, episode 1 (episode numbering follows the series' 2019–2020 release)
- Release: 23 December 2019 (episode guide); Spray Daily repost dated 5 February 2020
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mc3C6-ymuMs
- English-subtitle page: https://www.spraydaily.com/the-rise-of-graffiti-writing-dortmund-berlin-frankfurt/
- ILOVEGRAFFITI episode announcement: https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/2019/12/23/the-rise-of-graffiti-writing-from-new-york-to-europe-episode-17/

ILOVEGRAFFITI's announcement says the episode brings together first-generation protagonists including ATOM, SHARK, MASON, BOMBER, KENT and Marianne Brentzel, and introduces the early 1983/84 period. Spray Daily describes it as first-generation testimony about the spread of graffiti in Dortmund, Frankfurt and Berlin.

### B. *The Rise of Graffiti Writing — Writing on Trains: Dortmund & Frankfurt*
- Spray Daily repost date: 6 February 2020
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I48NNsPbpNM
- English-subtitle page: https://www.spraydaily.com/the-rise-of-graffiti-writing-writing-on-trains-dortmund-frankfurt-english-subtitles/

The page identifies BOMBER (Frankfurt) and SHARK (Dortmund) as central participants and describes Dortmund's mid-1980s rise. This is the strongest immediate video lead for SHARK's first-person account.

### C. The *Into the Nineties — Dortmund & Frankfurt* episode
ILOVEGRAFFITI's December 2019 series overview identifies a further episode as **Episode 21: 1989–1993 Into the Nineties — Dortmund & Frankfurt**, describing ATOM, SHARK, MASON, BOMBER and KENT reflecting on the late 1980s and early 1990s. The same overview says the Dortmund and Frankfurt scenes experienced an unusually strong European boom and discusses local style development around 1988/89.

Source: https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/2019/12/25/the-rise-of-graffiti-writing-from-new-york-to-europe-episodes-20-22/

The direct episode URL was not independently recovered in this pass. Do not assume the numbered episode and the separate “Writing on Trains” upload are identical; keep them as separate records until video IDs and contents are checked.

## 2. Cross-check with the scholarly citation

Harald Hinz's 2022 chapter “Formale Aspekte der Dortmunder Graffiti – eine lokalstilistische Einordnung” explicitly cites:
- SHARK, personal communication dated 30 January 2020;
- the documentary *The Rise of Graffiti Writing* (access date in the chapter: 8 February 2020).

This creates a strong triangulation route: Hinz's scholarly chapter points to a dated interview and documentary; ILOVEGRAFFITI and Spray Daily preserve episode announcements and accessible video URLs.

**Important:** this verifies the existence and provenance of the documentary route, not the exact content of any answer in the videos. The video must be watched and timecoded before quoting or attributing claims.

## 3. Recommended viewing and annotation protocol

For each episode, log:
1. exact episode title, URL, upload / broadcast date and subtitle language;
2. participant name exactly as introduced;
3. timecode for each historical claim;
4. whether the statement is first-person memory, second-hand recollection, interviewer framing or editorial narration;
5. date/place/person/crew mentioned and confidence;
6. whether the statement is corroborated by *Da kukse wa*, *ALL SURFACE*, Hinz, Lintzen, contemporary press or dated photographs;
7. contradictions or chronology drift;
8. rights and permissions for any still frame or transcript excerpt.

Do not generate a crew roster from everyone appearing in the documentary. Participation in an episode does not prove crew membership.

## 4. NIGHTSHIFT implications

- Treat the documentary as oral-history source material, not a visual style guide.
- Use timecoded testimony to verify chronology, travel, influences, train context and scene relationships.
- Keep personal recollection attributed to the speaker.
- Compare statements about Dortmund's local style with Hinz's three formal attributes (sharp contours, consistent stem thickness, opaque fill) and with the actual dated visual corpus.
- Do not infer exact glyph topology from a spoken description.
- Do not extract frames into NIGHTSHIFT without item-level rights review.
