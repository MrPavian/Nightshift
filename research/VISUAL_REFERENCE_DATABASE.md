## Institutional collections and documented visual material

### VR-001 — Martin Wong Graffiti Collection / Above Ground
- **Institution:** Museum of the City of New York
- **Scope:** More than 300 canvases and works on paper; includes works by Rammellzee, Lee Quiñones, Lady Pink and Futura 2000.
- **Historical relevance:** documents the movement of artists and visual approaches from subway/street contexts into canvas, paper, galleries and museums.
- **Record:** https://www.mcny.org/exhibition/above-ground-art-martin-wong-graffiti-collection
- **Rights:** museum collection; individual image rights must be checked. No blanket reuse permission inferred.
- **Evidence:** A

### VR-002 — Henry Chalfant: Art vs. Transit, 1977–1987
- **Institution:** The Bronx Museum
- **Scope:** photographs and historical ephemera documenting subway works, including pieces no longer extant.
- **Historical relevance:** train composition, panel segmentation, whole-train documentation, lettering and visual anthropology.
- **Record:** https://bronxmuseum.org/exhibition/henry-chalfant-art-vs-transit-1977-1987/
- **Rights:** images credited to photographers/archives; permission required unless a specific license says otherwise.
- **Evidence:** A

### VR-003 — Bronx Aerosol Arts Documentary Project
- **Institution:** Bronx County Historical Society, presented through Google Arts & Culture
- **Scope:** oral histories and tags from early writers including ROCKY 184, STAFF 161, TOPAZ 1, RIFF 170 and BUTCH 2.
- **Historical relevance:** firsthand testimony, early signatures and crew context.
- **Record:** https://artsandculture.google.com/story/-AVhMvHuARRJQA
- **Rights:** verify rights for each oral-history recording and tag image with the Bronx County Historical Society.
- **Evidence:** A

### VR-004 — Smithsonian Archives of American Art: Jack Stewart Papers
- **Institution:** Smithsonian Archives of American Art
- **Scope:** archival research files on graffiti; the Smithsonian record identifies Jack Stewart's papers and a research-files series.
- **Historical relevance:** research trail, interviews, transcripts and documentary material for New York graffiti history.
- **Record:** https://collections.si.edu/search/results.htm?fq=object_type%3A%22Archival+materials%22&q=%22New+York+City+Transit+Authority%22
- **Rights:** collection record states copyright and donor restrictions may apply; consult archive terms and item-level records.
- **Evidence:** A

### VR-005 — City as Canvas
- **Institution:** Museum of the City of New York
- **Scope:** 150 works on canvas, sketchbooks and photographs from the Martin Wong Collection, focusing on 1980s New York writing.
- **Historical relevance:** sketchbook practice, transition between walls/subways and art institutions, material culture.
- **Record:** https://www.mcny.org/exhibition/cityascanvas
- **Rights:** individual work and photograph rights must be verified.
- **Evidence:** A
