# Visual Reference Database

> Rights-aware research index. These records are not permission to reproduce images. Public access does not equal reuse permission.

## Use policy
1. Separate research access from copying or redistribution.
2. Preserve creator, photographer, date, location, archive and object identifiers where supplied.
3. Do not infer writer, crew, borough, date or style from appearance alone.
4. Separate visible observation, historical interpretation and NIGHTSHIFT design inference.
5. Prefer commissioned, licensed or NIGHTSHIFT-authored diagrams for shipped assets.
6. Do not use an individual writer's tag/signature or specific piece as a generative template. Extract broad structural principles only.
7. Item-level rights override collection-level assumptions. Unclear means no product use.

## Rights status vocabulary
- **Open license confirmed:** exact item license covers intended use.
- **Permission granted:** written permission covers the specific use.
- **Reference-only:** may be studied/cited; reproduction not cleared.
- **Permission required:** contact rights holder before reproduction.
- **Unknown / unresolved:** do not use in product.
- **Public domain confirmed:** evidence recorded; never assume from age alone.

## Visual analysis protocol
For each selected source image, create an analysis record before using it to inform construction. Do not store a derivative trace or crop unless permission allows it.

1. **Source facts:** caption, creator, photographer, date, place, collection/object ID.
2. **Visible geometry:** silhouette, stems, bowls, terminals, crossbars, counters, apertures, overlaps and extensions.
3. **Negative space:** enclosed counters, open apertures, inter-letter channels and margins.
4. **Composition:** baseline, cap-height tendency, width rhythm, slant, scale hierarchy, pair spacing and word silhouette.
5. **Execution/material:** outline/fill layers, highlights, drips, spray softness and surface seams; mark uncertainty when inferred from a photograph.
6. **Interpretation:** likely grammar or technique, confidence and alternative readings.
7. **NIGHTSHIFT inference:** abstract generator rule; do not attribute it to the historical artist without documentation.

## NIGHTSHIFT analysis matrix

| Dimension | Record / measure | Generator implication |
|---|---|---|
| Letter skeleton | stems, bowls, diagonals, terminals | preserve recognizable identity |
| Proportion | width, height, stroke mass | bounded style-dependent parameters |
| Counter topology | count, enclosure, orientation, clearance | validate negative space independently |
| Aperture | opening, direction, throat width | represent as open topology |
| Pair relationship | gap, overlap, axis, bridge, interlock | explicit relationship graph |
| Word rhythm | width sequence, slant, baseline movement | optimize after glyph identity |
| Negative-space channels | inter-letter and internal voids | detect pinches and accidental closures |
| Surface | seams, windows, doors, frame edges | constrain placement without deforming glyphs |
| Execution | outline/fill/highlight/spray evidence | separate rendering from semantic geometry |
| Confidence | source quality and ambiguity | avoid rules based on one image |

### VR-018 update — ontology, annotation method and reuse terms
- The official ontology and illustrated manual have now been inspected. INGRID records image level, context relation, graffiti type, lettering style, design elements, directionality, figure style, color, technique/material, language/function, place/date and source provenance.
- The manual says photo date/location are contributor-supplied; other annotations are based on visual inspection. The described workflow normally uses two annotators, one from linguistics and one from art history. The manual acknowledges interpretive ambiguity in style/genre terms.
- The 2023 *Scientific Data* article reports original images under CC BY-SA 4.0, generated metadata under CC0 and the knowledge graph under CC BY 4.0, based on contributor agreements transferring rights for public use to INGRID. Check the current license and credit data on each record; CC BY-SA reuse requires attribution and ShareAlike compliance.
- This clarifies the earlier general rights caution: INGRID is not simply “research-only”, but record-level compliance remains mandatory. No record has yet been inspected and no image reused.
