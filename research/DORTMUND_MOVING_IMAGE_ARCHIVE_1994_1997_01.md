# Dortmund Moving-Image Archive — Rolling Thunder, King of the Line and Graffiti Dortmund 1994 (W02.24)

**Date:** 2026-11-14  
**Scope:** recoverable media, provenance and access routes for Dortmund graffiti moving images and interactive documentation, circa 1994–1997.  
**Evidence rule:** catalogue/shop metadata, contemporary reviews and retrospective link references are kept distinct from direct inspection of the media. No film has been watched in this work pass.

## 1. Rolling Thunder 001 — physical VHS access lead

A specialist retailer currently lists a used VHS titled *Rolling Thunder 1* / *Rolling Thunder 001*:
- Retailer: Mr. Graffiti, product page https://www.mrgraffiti.nl/product/vhs-rolling-thunder-1-used/
- Displayed listing: €24.95 and “In stock” at the time checked; stock and price can change.
- Product metadata: Germany; 40 minutes; production credited as **MASmedia / SHARK TV**.
- Retailer description: Dortmund trainwriting, including wholetrains, wholecars and panelpieces; music credited to DJ Sek 902 of Dortmunder Union.
- The retailer gives no publication year, catalogue number, tape condition details beyond “used”, or evidence of a digital master.

The 1997 *BOMBER* #14 review independently describes *Rolling Thunder 001* as a 40-minute Dortmund train-action video and mentions three-car colour and eight-car silver wholetrain examples. Its list of writer names is an editorial review list, not an audited on-screen credit roll or crew roster.

**Assessment:** this is the strongest concrete acquisition/access lead found so far. The retailer's producer attribution is useful provenance metadata, but remains a seller-supplied description until confirmed from the cassette label, packaging, opening/closing credits or production records. Do not infer that the current used tape is a first pressing or that its contents are complete.

## 2. King of the Line — interactive CD-ROM

The same *BOMBER* #14 review describes *King of the Line* as a two-CD-ROM product by MASmedia, a Dortmund multimedia company run by MASON. The reviewer describes a Windows interface with an interactive station view, selectable timetable/directions, audio samples, breakbeats and short films, including a CAN2 character-painting clip and trains in motion.

A secondary product listing gives:
- Title: *King of the Line, 2 CD-ROMs: Graffiti. Für Windows 95 / NT*
- Publisher/brand: Edition Aragon
- ISBN 978-3-89535-458-8 / 3895354589
- Listing: https://www.preispiraten.de/preisvergleich/software/hobby%2B%26%2Bfreizeit/edition%2Baragon%2Bking%2Bof%2Bthe%2Bline%2B2%2Bcd%2Broms%2Bgraffiti%2Bf%C3%BCr%2Bwindows%2B95%2Bnt-ean-9783895354588

The product listing does not establish current availability, disc integrity, compatibility with modern systems, or the rights holder. Preserve the original disc images only through lawful acquisition and do not bypass copy protection. For research, prioritize screenshots, manuals, packaging and a documented, permission-cleared capture rather than distributing a disk image.

Its surviving archive text says the complete film was available through Google Video at the time of the post (approximately 18 years before the current check). The original Google Video destination has now been recovered from the post's embedded link: https://video.google.de/videoplay?docid=-5734405700324025569 (Google Video docid **-5734405700324025569**). Following that exact link currently returns HTTP 404. No verified surviving copy or mirror was located in this pass.

Archive route: https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/tag/dortmund/

**Assessment:** D-level discovery lead only. The title and historical Google Video reference are documented; the film's producer, duration, contents, authorship, date of upload and present availability remain unknown. Do not conflate this item with *Rolling Thunder 001*, the *Writing on Trains* documentary upload, or *The Rise of Graffiti Writing* episodes.

## 4. Implications for the NIGHTSHIFT / BLACKBOOK ARCHIVE work

1. Treat *Rolling Thunder 001* as a distinct moving-image source for train formats and Dortmund's mid/late-1990s scene, not as a representative sample of all Dortmund writing.
2. Treat *King of the Line* as an early interactive media/archive artifact. Its interface description is useful for studying historical navigation and media packaging, not as evidence that the source material is complete.
3. Keep *Graffiti Dortmund 1994* as an unresolved catalogue record until an identifiable copy or production credit is recovered.
4. No footage, stills, music, tags, pieces, or recognizable writer signatures are copied into NIGHTSHIFT. A purchased tape or disc does not itself grant reproduction, digitization, public display or commercial reuse rights.
5. Any future visual analysis must log source timecode/frame, visible train format, date/location only where evidenced, attribution confidence and rights status. Do not use identifiable living writers' work as generative templates.
