# Dortmund Graffiti Publications — 1996 Bibliographic Discrepancy and Later Visual Corpus (W02.25)

**Date:** 2026-11-14  
**Purpose:** distinguish similarly titled Dortmund publications and document current inspection/access limits. This note is a bibliography audit, not a visual analysis.

## 1. The 1996 *Graffiti Dortmund* title: page-count conflict

A 2009 ILOVEGRAFFITI post, “Wer ist eigentlich Chintz?”, describes a Markus Wiese book titled *Graffiti Dortmund* as **80 pages**, approximately 13 years old at the time (thus pointing to a 1996 publication). It describes the book as combining newspaper articles, old photographs and contributions by SHARK, Barbara Uduwerella and Bernhard van Treeck. The same post says a low-resolution Issuu turn-page version was then freely viewable.

- ILOVEGRAFFITI post (8 September 2009): https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/2009/09/08/wer-ist-eigentlich-chintz/
- The post's embedded “jump” link points to an Issuu document; the current web open attempt did not resolve the Issuu document.
- BERLIN GRAFFITI post (9 September 2009) independently identifies *Graffiti Dortmund – Die Kunst der Sachbeschädigung* as a 1996 book and links to the same Issuu upload: https://berlingraffiti.de/2009/09/09/graffiti-dortmund/

A catalogue/retail record for *Graffiti Dortmund: Die Kunst der Sachbeschädigung* identifies:
- Authors: Markus Wiese, Barbara Uduwerella and Bernhard van Treeck
- Publisher: Aragon
- Year: 1996
- ISBN: 978-3-89535-431-1 / 3-89535-431-7
- Reported extent: 160 pages in several retail catalogue records (e.g. https://diebuchsuche.at/buch-isbn-9783895354311.html); some seller records omit pagination.

**Finding:** the 80-page and 160-page claims refer to a highly likely same-title / same-author 1996 book because the 2009 post's approximate age, contributor names and subject match the catalogued ISBN record. But no physical copy, title page, colophon or scan pagination has been collated. Possible explanations (abridged edition, scan extent, catalogue error, or a different 80-page book) remain unverified. Do not silently normalize to either 80 or 160 pages. Record as **bibliographic discrepancy, unresolved**.

The 2009 Issuu availability statement documents that the upload was viewable at that time; it does not establish current availability, the uploader's authorization, or permission to download, reproduce or reuse images.

## 2. Keep the publications distinct

### *Da kukse wa* (1987)
Marianne Brentzel text and Hubert Moormann photographs; interviews with CHINTZ, SHARK, CHANA and ZODIAK. The 2015 ILOVEGRAFFITI retrospective describes Moormann as photographing the scene over two years and identifies the interviewees. This is a separate 1987 publication, not the 1996 Markus Wiese book.
- https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/2015/03/02/buch-da-kukse-wa-graffiti-in-dortmund-1987/

### *Graffiti Dortmund: Die Kunst der Sachbeschädigung* (1996)
Wiese / Uduwerella / van Treeck, Aragon, ISBN 978-3-89535-431-1. The accessible 2009 digital-viewing trail is a low-resolution Issuu embed, currently unresolved. No page-level visual claims are made here.

### *IRON CITY — Graffiti Dortmund* (2002)
A separate MASmedia bilingual illustrated book, 128 pages, 22.5 × 21.5 cm according to Hitzerot's listing. Its description advertises early images from 1983, colour panels from 1986, 1990s painted railway-system documentation, and a sequence spanning the 1980s, 1990s and early 2000s. The MASON shop page separately confirms 128 pages, four-colour print, hardcover and German/English; its description dates the coverage 1983–2002.
- Hitzerot listing: https://hitzerot.com/shop/iron-city/
- MASON shop listing: https://shop.mason.de/product/iron-city-graffiti-dortmund-2002-signed

The Hitzerot listing currently says out of stock; the MASON listing advertises a signed copy at €89. Neither listing constitutes page-level inspection or independent authentication of image captions.

### *MASON — The Essence of Writing 1986–2019* (2020)
A separate artist-focused retrospective; not interchangeable with *IRON CITY* or the 1996 city-history book. Its four-phase structure is a publication/editorial device until the underlying pages and rationale are reviewed.

### *RIO — The Process of Writing* (2023)
A separate 224-page bilingual hardcover monograph, ISBN 978-3-00-074818-9, limited to 500 copies per edition according to Hitzerot. Its publisher/shop description presents a chronological selection of RIO's work and says Dortmund was the principal target, with international travel material and essays by Robert Kaltenhäuser, FUME and Pietro Rivasi.
- https://hitzerot.com/shop/rio/

This is a potentially valuable later primary/artist-controlled visual corpus for one writer's career, but it is not a city-wide archive and should not be used to infer a universal Dortmund style. No images or pages were inspected.

## 3. Evidence and design implications

- **Bibliographic facts:** distinguish titles, dates, publishers, ISBNs, page extents and format. Where sources conflict, preserve the conflict.
- **Publisher/seller descriptions:** useful for locating the corpus and understanding intended scope; not substitutes for captions, colophons or page-level evidence.
- **Visual research:** once a legitimate copy is inspected, log each image independently: page number, visible format (tag / throw-up / piece / panel / wholecar / wholetrain / wall), support, location/date only if captioned or corroborated, credited writer only where explicit, and rights status.
- **Generator design:** do not infer letter anatomy, stroke order, palette, or style parameters from promotional copy. If formal observations are later made, abstract them into non-attributive properties and validate across multiple writers and periods. Never reproduce a living writer's signature, tag or identifiable piece.
