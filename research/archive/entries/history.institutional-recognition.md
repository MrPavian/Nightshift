# Galleries, collections and museums

> From early exhibitions in 1980 to museum shows today: how writing entered institutions — without becoming the same thing as the street.

## Introduction

Writers were shown in exhibitions early: Lady Pink was included at sixteen in the 1980 Fashion MODA exhibition *Graffiti Art Success for America* [c1]. The Museum of the City of New York reports that Martin Wong's Museum of American Graffiti opened in August 1989 [c2]. In 2024–2025 the museum's exhibition *Above Ground* framed its collection as a path from street and subway roots to institutional recognition [c3].

## Why it matters

Collections preserve books, letters and photographs that would otherwise be lost ([[object.blackbook|blackbooks]]). They also shape memory: who was collected and exhibited is often who gets remembered [c4].

## Not the same as

- **The end of street writing** — institutional attention ran alongside continuing street practice ([[history.clean-train-campaign|after 1989]]).
