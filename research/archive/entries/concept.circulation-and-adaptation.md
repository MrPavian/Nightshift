# Circulation and local adaptation

> New York writing travelled through films, books, music and visits — and every place that received it changed it, or already had writing of its own.

## Introduction

New York's writing culture became globally influential through films, books, photography, hip-hop music, travel and personal exchange. In several places, local forms of public writing already existed or developed by other routes [c1]. Films such as *Wild Style* and *Style Wars*, books, tours and fanzines were important channels for writing reaching Europe [c2].

## Why it matters

“Export” suggests passive copies. The better model is circulation: scenes selected, adapted, resisted and mixed what reached them [c3]. Four contrasting cases in this archive show different relationships: [[scene.amsterdam|Amsterdam]], [[scene.paris|Paris]], [[scene.sao-paulo|São Paulo]] and [[scene.china|China]].

## Not the same as

- **The scene itself** — a film, magazine or visit is a channel, not the local scene [c3].
- **A country checklist** — regional chapters are authored histories with their own sources, not one paragraph per nation [c3].
