# Letter anatomy

> A vocabulary for the parts of a letter — stems, bowls, bars, terminals, junctions — and how they work together.

## Introduction

To talk about a letter's construction, NIGHTSHIFT names its parts: a B has a stem, an upper and a lower bowl, a junction where the bowls meet and two enclosed counters [c1]. These names describe structure, not how a writer moved the can or marker [c2].

## Why it matters

Naming parts makes it possible to compare letters, discuss changes and test whether a letter keeps its identity when it is edited — for example, whether a B still has two counters (see [[construction.negative-space|negative space]]).

## In NIGHTSHIFT

The anatomy viewer and the editor show the same parts on the game's own letters. The component graph is an engineering model, not a claim that writers consciously build every letter this way [c2].
