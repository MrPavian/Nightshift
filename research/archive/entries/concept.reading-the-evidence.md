# How to read this archive

> Every statement here is marked: is it a dated record, a writer's memory, a researcher's interpretation, or how NIGHTSHIFT models something?

## Introduction

The archive separates four kinds of statements: documented facts, practitioner accounts, scholarly interpretations and NIGHTSHIFT design inferences [c1]. Each claim also carries an evidence label, and each entry has its own editorial status and image-rights status — three independent things [c2].

## How to recognize it

Tap a claim marker such as [c1] in an entry to see its kind, its evidence label and the sources behind it. Statements by writers about their own lives are valuable, and they are shown as their accounts.

## Why it matters

A photograph documents a work at one moment; a publication reprints it; an archive holds it — each step has its own date, author and rights [c3]. Publicly visible does not mean reusable, which is why this edition embeds no photographs of writers' work.
