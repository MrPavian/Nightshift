# Hall of Fame

> A wall where writers may paint legally or are tolerated — a place for time, detail and large pieces.

## Introduction

Halls of fame are common in the Ruhrgebiet. The KWI Essen article lists legal sites at the Ruhr-Universität in Bochum, in the Landschaftspark in Duisburg and in Essen, and describes a Dortmund harbour wall opened in April 2022 as the largest in North Rhine-Westphalia [c1].

## Why it matters

Legal walls give writers time to work on elaborate pieces ([[practice.piece|pieces]]), while tags and throw-ups elsewhere remain contested [c2]. Both belong to the same culture; a hall of fame is one setting among several, not the “acceptable” half of writing.

## Not the same as

- **A commissioned mural** — a hall of fame is usually open to writers on their own terms rather than a commissioned image.
