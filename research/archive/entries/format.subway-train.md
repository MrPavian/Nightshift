# The subway train

> In New York the subway became a moving display: work painted in one place travelled through the whole city.

## Introduction

The subway system became a major moving display and communication infrastructure for New York writing [NYC-007]. Scholarship describes subway writing as turning transportation infrastructure into a communication technology [c1].

## Why it matters

A train carried a writer's name beyond the neighbourhood where it was painted, which changed the scale of visibility and audience. The subway should be understood as an audience network, not only as a convenient surface [c2].

## Context and development

The city's campaign against painted trains is a related but separate history [NYC-008] (see [[history.clean-train-campaign|Clean Train campaign]]). Because painted cars rarely survived, photographs and films became the main record [NYC-009].

## Not the same as

- **The only surface** — wall writing predates and coexists with subway writing [c2].
