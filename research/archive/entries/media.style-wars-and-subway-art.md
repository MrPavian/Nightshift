# Style Wars and Subway Art

> A 1983 documentary and a 1984 photo book that carried New York writing far beyond New York.

## Introduction

Photography, film and publications preserved temporary work and spread knowledge beyond the places where it was made [NYC-009]. In the 1980s, the international visibility of New York writing was accelerated by documentary and print media, notably *Style Wars* and *Subway Art* [NYC-010].

## Why it matters

For many people outside New York, these media were the first contact with writing. They became reference points for later scenes — which then developed in their own directions (see [[concept.circulation-and-adaptation|circulation]]).

## Context and development

Henry Chalfant, whose sequential photographs record whole trains, co-produced *Style Wars* (1983) [c1].

## Not the same as

- **The scene itself** — a film or book is a channel of transmission, not the scene [c2].
- **The original artwork** — a photograph documents a work at one moment; it is not the work [c3].
