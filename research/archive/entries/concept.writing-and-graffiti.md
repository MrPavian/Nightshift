# Writing and graffiti

> “Writing” is the practitioners’ word for a specific culture of putting a chosen name into public space; “graffiti” is a much wider umbrella.

## Introduction

The word *graffiti* covers many unrelated practices. In this archive, the New York chapter is about modern, name-centred hip-hop writing [NYC-001]. Writers and scholars often use *writing* or *graffiti writing* for that specific culture, while *graffiti* is the broader public label [c1].

## Why it matters

The distinction keeps very different histories apart: ancient inscriptions, political slogans, gang marks and modern name writing are not one practice ([[concept.public-inscription|public inscription]]).

## Terminology and variants

Usage varies by person, place and period. The archive does not police every author's terminology; it records which meaning a source uses. In German-speaking research the term *Style Writing* is common.

## Not the same as

- **Street art** — related and overlapping, but murals, stencils, stickers and name-centred writing have different histories [c2].
- **Public inscription in general** (→ concept.public-inscription) — writing is one modern culture within a much longer history of marks in public space.
