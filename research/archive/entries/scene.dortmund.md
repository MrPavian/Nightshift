# Dortmund and the Ruhrgebiet

> Dortmund was the first city in the Ruhrgebiet with writers working inside a hip-hop environment, and its blocky letters became known well beyond the region.

## Introduction

A 2022 article for the Kulturwissenschaftliches Institut Essen describes writing as an art form that emerged on the US East Coast, in New York and Philadelphia, and spread internationally, notably through the film *Wild Style*; it places the establishment of graffiti in the Ruhrgebiet in the mid-1980s [c1]. The same article says Dortmund has always had a special role in the region and was the first Ruhrgebiet city where writers were active in a hip-hop environment. It names Chintz, Zodiak and Shark, all members of the TUF crew (The United Force) [c2].

## Why it matters

Dortmund shows how a German scene received writing through film and hip-hop and then built its own recognisable letter language ([[style.dortmund-style|Dortmund Style]]) [c3]. It is a chapter in its own right, not a footnote to “European influence” [c4].

## Context and development

Today the region has many legal walls (see [[practice.hall-of-fame|Hall of Fame]]), while tags and throw-ups remain unwelcome to much of the public and to city authorities [c5].

## Evidence notes
