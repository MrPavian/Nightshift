# Throw-up and piece

> Beside the tag, two larger forms: the quick, outlined throw-up and the composed, multi-layered piece.

## Introduction

Ethnographic research on New York writers describes tags, throw-ups and pieces as common forms of the culture [c1]. A throw-up is fast and outlined; a piece takes more time and builds a composition from fill, outline and further layers [c2].

## How to recognize it

Throw-ups usually use a few rounded or simplified letters with outline and quick fill. Pieces show planning: letter construction, connections between letters, colour and depth [c2].

## Not the same as

- **Steps on a ladder** — tag, throw-up and piece coexist; a writer is not expected to “graduate” from one to the next [c3].
