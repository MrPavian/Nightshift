## What the records establish — and what they do not

- These two exact catalogue records establish that the MCL catalogue lists the titles and displays the stated holding information.
- The *Eine Stadt wird bunt* record is a strong research pathway for Hamburg scene history; the catalogue description alone does not verify every historical claim or identify which images may be reused.
- The Kaiser title is useful as a secondary contextual source, but its presence in the catalogue does not make it an authoritative source for individual writer biographies or crew membership.
- No holding is claimed here for *Daily Bombs*, *Dosenspuk*, *Beastie Boyz*, *BACKSPIN*, *Sketches*, *Rock 'n' Roller* or any particular *Stylefile* issue. Those titles require their own exact catalogue records.
- A catalogue record is not a reproduction licence. Image and text rights remain item- and use-specific.

## Access and catalogue limitations

The MCL describes its collection as specializing in literature on urban art, street art and graffiti since the 1960s, with a substantial focus on style writing from the 1970s onward in the US tradition. Its institutional information explains that holdings are kept in closed stacks and requested for use in the reading room; the OPAC can be searched remotely, but on-site use requires registration/confirmed access. The library is a reference collection, not a lending service.

Sources:
- [Martha Cooper Library — collection and use information](https://urban-nation.com/de/ueber-uns/martha-cooper-library/)
- [Martha Cooper Library — general information](https://open.urban-nation.com/de-de/%C3%9Cber-uns/Allgemeine-Informationen)

## NIGHTSHIFT implications

1. Add *Eine Stadt wird bunt* to the high-priority Hamburg source pathway and consult the catalogue record's linked publisher/contents material before making claim-level assertions.
2. Use the MCL OPAC as a discovery tool, not as proof that an unlocated title or issue is held.
3. Maintain separate fields for catalogue existence, physical holding, current availability, inspected content and image reuse rights.
4. For archive entries, cite the publication actually consulted, not merely its catalogue listing.
5. Do not treat catalogue metadata or an online cover as permission to reproduce the cover or interior images in NIGHTSHIFT.
