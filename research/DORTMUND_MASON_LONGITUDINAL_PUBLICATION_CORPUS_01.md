Bibliographic metadata found across specialist and bookseller catalogues:
- Title: *Graffiti Dortmund: Die Kunst der Sachbeschädigung*
- Names listed: Markus Wiese; Barbara Uduwerella; Bernhard van Treeck
- Publisher: Aragon, Moers
- Year: 1996
- ISBN: 978-3-89535-431-1
- Extent: 160 pages (catalogue listings vary on publication year / edition details)
- Language: German

A 2009 Berlin Graffiti post records a link to an Issuu scan and says that a freely accessible PDF copy had been found by Graffiti Magazine. This verifies a historical online access route, **not** authorization by the rights holders. Issuu access did not resolve in this research pass. Treat the scan as an unverified third-party upload and do not extract, redistribute or reproduce it.

Sources:
- 2009 scan notice: https://berlingraffiti.de/2009/09/09/graffiti-dortmund/
- ISBN / contributor catalogue record: https://www.zvab.com/Graffiti-Dortmund-Kunst-Sachbesch%C3%A4digung-Wiese-Markus/32467980006/bd

**Research value:** likely an important 1990s visual and scene-history source. The KIT dissertation cites it repeatedly for early Dortmund chronology and trainwriting; Hinz (2022) also compares specific images in it with other historical publications. It should be inspected through a legitimate physical/library copy before page-level claims are made.

## 2. *MASON — The Essence of Writing 1986–2019* (2020)

ILOVEGRAFFITI's 2 February 2020 publication announcement records:
- publication date announced: 17 February 2020;
- 144 pages;
- numbered limited edition of 400;
- chronological selection of MASON's work from 1986 to 2019;
- four work phases;
- accompanying texts by Robert Kaltenhäuser, François Chastanet and MOSES.

The post includes an Issuu preview embed, but the embed did not resolve in this research pass. The preview's existence is documented; its pages were not inspected.

Source: https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/2020/02/02/mason-the-essence-of-writing-1986-2019/

This catalogue is a particularly relevant companion to Rahel H. Grothus's 2022 *Dirty Hands* chapter, “Stilbeschreibung des Œuvres des Künstlers Mason” (pp. 269–278). Grothus's abstract says that the chapter divides MASON's signature development into four time periods from 1986 to 2020 and combines artist conversations with comparison of artworks.

The two sources form a useful longitudinal study pair:
- 2020 catalogue: curated chronology and selected visual works through 2019;
- 2022 chapter: art-historical analysis of continuity and change through 2020, with artist conversations.

Neither is a generic Dortmund style template. MASON is an individual case.

## 4. NIGHTSHIFT use

- Build a source timeline before any MASON-related analysis: item date, publication date, work phase and creator credit must remain separate.
- Distinguish signature studies from pieces, wholecars and train panels; a longitudinal tag/signature corpus cannot automatically stand in for the full body of work.
- Use four-phase periodization only as an attributed catalogue/chapter structure until the underlying selection and rationale are reviewed.
- Any extracted geometric observations must be abstract and non-attributive. Do not copy MASON's signature, distinctive letterforms or identifiable pieces.
- Keep all image rights unresolved until the actual rights holder and permitted uses are documented.
