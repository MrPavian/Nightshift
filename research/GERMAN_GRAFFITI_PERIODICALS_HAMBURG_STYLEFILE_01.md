# German Graffiti Periodicals — Hamburg Network & Stylefile Continuity 01

**Research date:** 2026-11-14  
**Scope:** Hamburg's early-1990s graffiti periodicals and the Stylefile publishing line, including the 2025–2026 restart.  
**Evidence discipline:** Publisher/editorial statements, contemporary specialist coverage and retrospective magazine catalogues are kept distinct. Dates and issue runs are not inferred from a title list alone.

## 1. Hamburg's editorial network: Daily Bombs, Dosenspuk, Beastie Boyz and Backspin

The official EINE STADT WIRD BUNT editor biographies provide unusually useful scene attribution:

- **Andreas Timm / Cario:** active in Hamburg writing since 1988; publisher of Daily Bombs from 1992 to 1999; later a long-standing member of the Backspin magazine team.
- **Oliver Nebel / Davis One:** active writer since 1988; publisher of Dosenspuk from 1992 and Beastie Boyz until 2001; worked for Backspin until 2009.
- **Frank Petering:** active in Hamburg writing from 1986 to 1995; identified as one of the founders and publishers of Backspin.
- **Mirko Reisser:** active writer since 1989; his biography provides a bridge between Hamburg writing, art education and later institutional/exhibition work, but does not attribute him as editor of the periodicals above.

Source: EINE STADT WIRD BUNT, official editor biographies, https://einestadtwirdbunt.de/infos/herausgeber/ (accessed 2026-11-14).

### What this supports

This is strong evidence for overlapping editorial labour and scene participation. It supports modelling magazines as part of a connected cultural/media network rather than isolated publication brands. It does **not** by itself establish complete editorial staffs, contributor rosters, circulation, issue counts or exact publication frequency.

## 2. Daily Bombs #13 — contemporary continuity, not a simple reprint claim

ILOVEGRAFFITI.DE reported on 16 January 2026 that issue #13 appeared at the end of 2025, 33 years after the first issue in 1992. The reported contents include Hamburg walls, trains, DHL vehicles, a photo feature on the 13ERN, street bombing and trains, plus named sections and a retrospective black-and-white segment.

This documents a contemporary continuation of a publication rooted in the early-1990s Hamburg scene. It does not prove uninterrupted publication between 1992 and 2025. For NIGHTSHIFT's archive, this is a useful example of a scene publication reappearing as a memory-bearing editorial object, with contemporary material and retrospective visual language in one issue.

Source: https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/2026/01/16/daily-bombs-graffiti-magazin-ausgabe-13/

## 3. Stylefile: original run, interruption and 2025–2026 return

### Founding and publishing context

Publikat Press states that the first Stylefile Magazine appeared in 1999 and describes the magazine as the foundation of its subsequent publishing activity. The publisher says it produced and distributed more than 100 titles between 1999 and 2017. Its current account says the founders Markus Christl and Jörn Stiller left after the company's e-commerce difficulties, the later owner failed to stabilize the business, and Markus Christl regained the STYLEFILE MAGAZINE and PUBLIKAT trademark rights in 2025, restarting the brand after seven years away.

Source: Publikat Press, About, https://www.publikat-press.com/pages/about (accessed 2026-11-14).

This is the publisher's own retrospective account. It is appropriate for the company's stated history and trademark restart, but should be attributed as publisher testimony rather than treated as an independent corporate-history audit.

### Issue 62 — ROBOTfile

The official product page identifies Stylefile #62 — ROBOTfile, with a release date of **1 November 2026**, 96 pages, English, softcover. Its product description names a SEEN interview as the headline feature and lists THE POSSE, ZEUS40, RUEDIONE and RADICALS among the contents/featured names.

Source: https://www.publikat-press.com/products/stylefile-62-robotfile (checked 2026-11-14).

This gives a precise current endpoint for the issue chronology: #61 in 2025 and #62 on 1 November 2026. It does not fill the historical gap or verify every earlier issue.

The current Publikat Press magazine collection lists 63 products and includes selected back issues such as #15, #19, #22, #25, #33–35, #40, #43–47, #49–53, #61 and #62. The live assortment is a sales catalogue, not a complete historical bibliography. Missing numbers must not be interpreted as unpublished or unavailable elsewhere.

## 4. Editorial and product implications for NIGHTSHIFT

1. **Scene networks:** represent editors, writers, publications, contributors and institutions as separate entity types connected by dated, sourced relationships. Do not turn publisher-of into member-of or assume all contributors were writers.
2. **Magazine chronology:** store issue-level dates and identifiers separately from title-level active periods. An issue 13 in 2025 does not imply annual or continuous publication.
3. **Media ecology:** Backspin should be represented as a broader hip-hop magazine with a graffiti-connected editorial network, not flattened into the same category as a dedicated graffiti zine.
4. **Archive entry status:** the Hamburg editor links are suitable for attributed draft entries. Claims about individual issue contents, circulation, format, contributors and visual style require issue-level evidence.
5. **Stylefile current status:** #61 and #62 are contemporary official publisher records; they can anchor a 2025–2026 timeline, but should not be used to generalize the entire 1999–2017 back catalogue.
6. **Image rights:** covers and interior pages are copyrighted unless an explicit licence or permission says otherwise. A shop page or magazine photograph is a provenance pointer, not permission to copy it into the game.
7. **Generator relevance:** publications can be used as scene/context references and curated learning pathways. Do not derive a style grammar from cover art alone; inspect multiple attributed examples and distinguish individual writers' work from editorial layout.
