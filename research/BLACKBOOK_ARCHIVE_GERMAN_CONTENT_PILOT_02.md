# THE BLACKBOOK ARCHIVE — German Scene Content Pilot 02

**Status:** editorial drafts; not publication-approved. Based on GERMAN_SCENE_ADDITIONAL_LEADS_01.md. Unresolved leads remain excluded.

## Entry DE-10 — Stylefile Magazine
- ID: publication.stylefile
- Type: Magazine / scene documentation / media infrastructure
- Definition: German graffiti magazine founded in 1999 in Aschaffenburg, documenting European and international graffiti culture.
- Context: Publikat identifies Stylefile as its foundational publication and describes its editorial approach as from the scene for the scene. Its archive spans more than 60 issues; trademark rights returned to founder Markus Christl in 2025 and a new chapter was announced. [SL-01; SL-03]
- Distinguish from: crew, style or shop; Stylefile is a publication and historical source.
- Learning angle: issue-by-issue timeline of trains, walls, blackbooks, city reports, interviews and photography.
- Rights: magazine scans and photographs not cleared by catalogue availability. Status: draft.

## Entry DE-11 — 1UP / One United Power
- ID: crew.1up
- Type: Graffiti crew / collective identity
- Definition: Berlin crew described as founded in Kreuzberg in 2003, known for painting under one shared crew name and documenting collective actions.
- Context: The Writers Bench Collection records the crew's history and its collaboration with Martha Cooper on *One Week with 1UP* (2018). [SL-04]
- Distinguish from: Berlin Kidz as a film/scene phenomenon; do not infer complete membership overlap.
- Learning angle: collective identity, crew name, documentation and international circulation.
- Rights: crew imagery requires creator/photographer clearance. Status: draft.

## Entry DE-12 — Berlin Kidz
- ID: media.berlin-kidz
- Type: Film / documentary representation / Berlin scene phenomenon
- Definition: A film/DVD documenting a particular Berlin urban-action milieu and its visual representation.
- Context: A 2014 Berlin Graffiti listing describes the film's content and tags 1UP; this is insufficient to establish a formal crew roster or exact affiliations. [SL-05]
- Distinguish from: 1UP as a crew with a separate history.
- Learning angle: compare film editing, documentation, media image and historical testimony.
- Safety: no interactive replication of train surfing, rooftop descents or dangerous actions. Status: draft.

## Entry DE-13 — MOSES & TAPS
- ID: collective.moses-and-taps
- Type: Artist collective / conceptual graffiti-related practice
- Definition: Collective founded by Moses and Taps in 2007, with roots in 1990s graffiti and public-space work dating to 1994, according to URBAN NATION.
- Context: Institutional biography describes an evolving collective and works including the realistic train door (2010) and *The Wall* (2015). [SL-06]
- Distinguish from: classic letterform grammar; its practice includes conceptual and illusion-based work.
- Learning angle: context, perception, train iconography and post-graffiti.
- Status: draft.

## Entry DE-14 — LOOMIT
- ID: writer.loomit
- Type: Writer / Munich scene
- Definition: Munich writer who says he began painting in July 1983.
- Context: In a 2009 interview he names FBI, UA and FX as crews and describes early influences including Lee, Zephyr and Dondi after seeing *Wild Style*. [SL-07]
- Distinguish from: a generic “Munich style” or a fixed preset.
- Learning angle: first-person chronology, travel and international exchange, development of spatial lettering.
- Status: draft.

## Entry DE-15 — OZ
- ID: writer.oz-hamburg
- Type: Writer / Hamburg urban visual intervention
- Definition: Walter Josef Fischer (1950–2014), known as OZ, whose marks, smileys and symbols became a recurring part of Hamburg's urban visual environment.
- Context: City of OZ, a project by Gängeviertel e.V. and the Graffitiarchiv / Archiv der Jugendkulturen e.V., documents his life, work, press coverage and contested reception. Scholarly work analyses his smileys as a distinctive visual project. [SL-08; SL-09; SL-10]
- Distinguish from: conventional name-writing alone; his practice included tags, smileys, spirals and other symbols.
- Editorial approach: preserve both cultural significance and the conflict around property damage, repression, solidarity and public space. Avoid sensationalist statistics and romanticization of danger.
- Status: draft.

## Entry DE-16 — CAN2
- ID: writer.can2
- Type: Writer / first-person testimony
- Definition: German writer who says he painted his first piece in December 1983.
- Context: A 2010 interview describes his path from B-boying and music videos to graffiti, sketching and airbrush. [SL-11]
- Distinguish from: unverified city, crew or career assumptions.
- Learning angle: connect hip-hop media, sketch culture and technique through attributed testimony.
- Status: draft.
