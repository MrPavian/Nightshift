# German Writers, Crews and Networks — Research Register 01

**Status:** initial source-backed register; not a definitive crew directory.  
**Rule:** aliases, crew memberships, dates and contributions are recorded only with attribution and period scope. A writer may belong to different crews at different times.

## Dortmund / Ruhrgebiet

### CHINTZ
- Research context: early Dortmund scene and Dortmund Style.
- The KWI Essen overview names CHINTZ alongside ZODIAK and SHARK as part of the early Dortmund environment. The KIT dissertation discusses CHINTZ and the TUF crew in relation to Dortmund train writing and regional style.
- Sources: DE-01, DE-03.
- Open: dated work examples, crew membership chronology, first-person testimony.

### SHARK
- Research context: early Dortmund scene; named in KWI and scholarly regional histories.
- Sources: DE-01, DE-03, DE-04.
- Open: alias verification, crew chronology, dated work.

### ZODIAK
- Research context: early Dortmund scene and hip-hop graffiti development.
- Sources: DE-03, DE-04.
- Open: primary documentation and first-person account.

### ATOM ONE / Der Lange
- Research context: Dortmund writer and Too Strong member; associated with TRD in the ILOVEGRAFFITI podcast description.
- Podcast #040 discusses early graffiti, Dortmund Style, New York travel, Amsterdam influences and generational relationships.
- Sources: DE-05; ILOVEGRAFFITI article on ATOM ONE's work: https://ilovegraffiti.de/blog/2017/03/11/the-real-deal-a-few-graffiti-pieces-by-atom-one/
- Open: independently verify dated career milestones and membership periods.

### Pure Doze / DOZE
- Research context: Too Strong founding member and interviewee on Silo Nation's origins.
- Source: DE-06.
- Important: do not conflate Pure Doze with ATOM ONE / Der Lange. They are separate people.

### Silo Nation
- Classification: hip-hop community/network and identity, not automatically a graffiti crew.
- DOZE and ATOM describe the origin as a 1993 Dortmund gathering/celebration that developed a shared identity and a recurring jam culture. Theater Dortmund presents it as a lifestyle/community spanning hip-hop elements.
- Sources: DE-06, DE-07.
- Open: collect flyers, event photographs, recordings, oral histories and participant accounts; distinguish original joke/wordplay from later formal meanings.

### TUF / TRD
- TUF appears in the KIT dissertation's account of Dortmund's 1990s train-writing environment.
- TRD is associated with ATOM ONE / Der Lange in the ILOVEGRAFFITI podcast description.
- Sources: DE-01, DE-05.
- Open: exact expansions, formation dates, membership and distinction between similarly named crews require direct verification.

## Berlin

### TDC — The Denots Crew; TCF — The Comic Freezers
- Research context: West Berlin crews in the 1980s.
- The Pop- und Subkulturarchiv names both in its overview of early West Berlin crew formation.
- Source: DE-08.
- Open: member lists and dated photographs.

### ODEM
- Research context: Berlin writer associated with the late-1980s/early-1990s transition to train writing in a retrospective BerlinWriters account; subject of Jürgen Deppe's 2003 book *On the Run*.
- Sources: DE-09, DE-10.
- Caution: the book is a situated narrative, not an independent chronology of all Berlin graffiti.
- Open: direct interview, contemporaneous publications and visual documentation.

### 1UP — One United Power
- Research context: later Berlin crew founded in Kreuzberg in 2003 according to The Writers Bench Collection.
- Known for collective use of the crew name and collaboration with Martha Cooper on *One Week with 1UP*.
- Source: DE-11.
- Caution: distinct generation and practice from 1980s West Berlin crews.

## Hamburg

### DAIM / Mirko Reisser
- Research context: Hamburg scene since 1989; internationally recognized for sustained experimentation with the four-letter name and three-dimensional lettering.
- University of Hamburg CSMC profile and research project document both his artistic practice and his private archive.
- Sources: DE-12, DE-13.
- Open: identify dated works, collaborators, crews and source images with item-level provenance.

## Editorial safeguards
- Do not turn the register into a “greatest writers” ranking.
- Do not infer crew membership from a photograph or a shared wall.
- Record membership by date range and source, including uncertainty.
- Distinguish crew, music group, hip-hop organization, jam, studio collective and commercial brand.
- Use first-person interviews as situated testimony and corroborate precise dates/claims where possible.
- Link visual examples only after creator, photographer, date, place and rights are documented.
