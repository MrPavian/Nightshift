# Hamburg Graffiti & Hip-Hop Magazines — Bibliography 01

**Research date:** 14 November 2026  
**Status:** source-graded serial-publication register; issue-level records remain incomplete.

## Why this matters

*EINE STADT WIRD BUNT* and the Martha Cooper Library both emphasize the role of scene-produced “grey literature”: magazines assembled from photographs collected and exchanged by participants. These publications are not just coverage; they are part of how scenes documented themselves, built networks and preserved otherwise ephemeral work. The MCL's essay on the Hamburg book specifically notes that scene magazines began appearing in the early 1990s and that four Hamburg editors later combined their archives for the 2021 book. [HM-01]

## 1. Daily Bombs (Hamburg)

- Type: graffiti magazine / serial publication.
- First issue: 1992, according to a graffiti-media timeline.
- Editor: Andreas Timm / Cario; official *EINE STADT WIRD BUNT* biography says he edited one of Hamburg's first graffiti magazines from 1992 to 1999.
- Grafflibrary currently records Daily Bombs as 1992–2025 and lists it among the longest-running magazine titles. The endpoint and total run need issue-level verification; it may include later revival/metadata conventions and should not be treated as proof of continuous publication. [HM-02; HM-03; HM-04]

**Archive record status:** serial title and editor-period supported; exact issue list, gaps, publication frequency and continuity unresolved.

## 2. Dosenspuk / Dosen Spuk (Hamburg)

- Type: graffiti magazine / serial publication.
- First issue: 1992, according to the media timeline.
- Editor: Oliver Nebel / Davis One; official project biography says he edited Dosenspuk from 1992.
- Spelling varies between “Dosenspuk” and “Dosen Spuk” in sources; retain a canonical title only after inspecting masthead/colophon, and preserve alternate forms as aliases. [HM-02; HM-03; HM-04]

**Archive record status:** title and editor association supported; issue count, exact title styling, publication period and contents unresolved.

## 3. Beastie Boyz / Boys / Boy'z (Hamburg)

Grafflibrary's publication record says the title emerged in 1993/1994, focused mainly on Hamburg, appeared every two months in black and white until 1997, then added 24 colour pages and increased its print run. In September 1998 it adopted a new format with 40 pages of photographs from Hamburg, Berlin, the USA, Spain and Australia. The title varied between “Boys”, “Boy'z” and “Boyz”; the record also notes six sketch-only issues and four different covers for issue 16. [HM-05]

The official *EINE STADT WIRD BUNT* editor biography identifies Oliver Nebel / Davis One as editor until 2001. [HM-03]

**Archive record status:** substantial publication history from a specialist catalogue, supported by editor biography; exact issue chronology and title variants should be checked against physical copies.

## 4. BACKSPIN (Hamburg)

- Type: hip-hop magazine covering the four elements, including graffiti.
- Founders: Bodo Falk and Frank Petering.
- The official *EINE STADT WIRD BUNT* biography identifies Frank Petering as a founder/editor.
- A 2019 anniversary interview with the founders says the magazine began in 1994, ran 102 issues under the founders and reached print runs of up to 70,000. [HM-03; HM-06]
- A historical magazine account gives August 1994 for issue 1, an initial print run of 3,000, 44 pages and 12 colour graffiti pages. It reports changes in frequency and format in 1997–1999. This account is useful but should be cross-checked against issue mastheads. [HM-07]
- A separate timeline lists “Backspin (Hamburg) – Nr. 1, 1993”, which conflicts with the 1994 founder testimony and issue-history account. [HM-08]

**Editorial rule:** do not silently reconcile 1993 vs 1994. Use “founded/developed in 1993” only if clearly attributed to the timeline; “first issue August 1994” is supported by the detailed issue history and founder anniversary account. Verify with the original issue's masthead.

## 5. Sketches (Hamburg)

The graffiti-media timeline lists *Sketches* (Hamburg), issue 1 in 1992. This is currently a single-source lead; do not infer publisher, editor, issue count or relation to Beastie Boyz' sketch-only supplements. [HM-08]

## 6. Rock 'n' Roller (Hamburg)

The same timeline lists *Rock 'n' Roller* (Hamburg), issue 1 at the end of 1991. No corroborating title-level or issue-level source was located in this pass. Keep as an unresolved bibliography lead, not a fully documented publication. [HM-08]

## 7. Publication relationships and editorial cautions

- These magazines are distinct serial publications; common editors or scene networks do not make them one publication.
- Do not assume every photograph in a scene magazine was taken by its editor or by the writer depicted.
- A magazine's editorial period is not necessarily its complete publication run.
- “Hamburg magazine” describes a place/network of production, not necessarily the geographic scope of all content.
- A scene-produced publication can be historically important while its claims still require attribution and corroboration.
- Copyright in photographs, scans, layouts and cover art must be checked item by item. A library catalogue or public-facing book description does not grant reproduction rights.
