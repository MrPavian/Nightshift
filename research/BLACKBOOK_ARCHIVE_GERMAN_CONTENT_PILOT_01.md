## Entry DE-01 — Dortmund Style
- ID: style.dortmund-block
- Type: Regional letterform tendency / historical style context
- Short definition: A regional lettering tendency associated with Dortmund and the Ruhrgebiet, described in research as comparatively simple, readable, angular and block-based, often shown in black/silver.
- Context: KIT research and KWI Essen describe the Dortmund Style as an identifiable regional development with influence beyond the Ruhrgebiet. The label must remain tied to dated examples and source context. [DE-01; DE-03]
- Recognize by: block construction, angular corners, clear silhouette, compact internal structure and a strong relationship between width, height and readability. These are analysis prompts, not universal rules.
- Distinguish from: generic blockbuster lettering; regional style label does not mean every Dortmund writer used the same construction.
- Visual learning: compare multiple dated writers and examples; annotate common tendencies and individual departures.
- Generator: future regional structural study only; never copy one writer's signature.
- Rights: use commissioned diagrams or cleared images only. Status: draft.

## Entry DE-02 — Silo Nation
- ID: culture.silo-nation
- Type: Hip-hop community / network / event culture
- Short definition: A Dortmund-rooted hip-hop identity and community associated with Too Strong and a recurring jam culture.
- Context: DOZE and ATOM describe its beginnings in 1993 around a gathering for Der Lange's birthday, with the name arising as wordplay on Zulu Nation/Silo. Theater Dortmund presents it as a lifestyle/community spanning rap, DJing, breakdance, graffiti/street art and beatboxing. [DE-06; DE-07]
- Distinguish from: a graffiti crew or a single music group. Do not classify it as either without further source-specific qualification.
- Learning angle: show networks across music, graffiti, jams, friendships and local identity.
- Generator: none; this is cultural context, not a style preset.
- Editorial caution: preserve participant accounts and changing meanings over time. Status: draft.

## Entry DE-03 — Too Strong
- ID: crew.too-strong
- Type: Hip-hop music group / scene network
- Short definition: A Dortmund hip-hop group with close connections to the local graffiti and jam environment.
- Context: ILOVEGRAFFITI's ATOM ONE episode describes the group's 1989 founding by DJ Zonic and Pure Doze, with DJ Brocke and Der Lange joining soon afterwards. Interviews connect the group with Silo Nation and Dortmund scene networks. [DE-05; DE-06]
- Distinguish from: TRD and other graffiti crews; musical group and graffiti affiliations are separate relationships.
- Related: Silo Nation, ATOM ONE / Der Lange, Pure Doze / DOZE, Dortmund Style.
- Rights: music, lyrics, photographs and logos require separate rights clearance. Status: draft.

## Entry DE-06 — DAIM and three-dimensional letterform research
- ID: writer.daim
- Type: Writer / letterform development
- Short definition: Mirko Reisser, known as DAIM, is a Hamburg writer whose work has explored the four-letter name DAIM through changing, highly spatial and three-dimensional forms.
- Context: The University of Hamburg CSMC documents his Hamburg scene history from 1989, first piece in 1989 and commissioned work in 1990, and describes his long-running formal exploration. [DE-12]
- Distinguish from: a generic 3D effect or a fixed DAIM preset. The archive should study broad spatial principles, not replicate his signature.
- Related: Hamburg writing, photography/archive, 3D lettering, getting-up.
- Status: draft.

## Entry DE-07 — Ma'Claim
- ID: crew.maclaim
- Type: Graffiti crew / collaborative practice
- Short definition: A German crew formed in 2001 around photorealistic aerosol character painting and collaborative visual production.
- Context: AKUT's first-person account describes the development from earlier collaborations among AKUT, CASE and RUSK and TASSO's invitation to form Ma'Claim with CASE and AKUT; RUSK joined the developing group. TASSO identifies earlier photorealistic aerosol pioneers and rejects an invention myth. [DE-14; DE-15]
- Distinguish from: classic letterform grammar and DAIM's 3D letterform work.
- Learning angle: digital planning, photographic reference translation, aerosol realism, crew specialization and collaboration.
- Generator: future material/character study, separate from canonical glyph construction.
- Rights: no tracing of protected photo references or Ma'Claim works. Status: draft.

## Entry DE-08 — ODEM: On the Run
- ID: writer.odem
- Type: Writer / first-person scene narrative
- Short definition: Berlin writer and subject of Jürgen Deppe's book *Odem: On the Run — Eine Jugend in der Graffiti-Szene* (2003).
- Context: A BerlinWriters retrospective names ODEM among writers associated with the late-1980s move toward painting trains; Deppe's book offers a situated narrative of early-1990s Berlin scene life. [DE-09; DE-10]
- Distinguish from: a complete or neutral history of Berlin writing. Memoir/biographical narrative must remain attributed.
- Learning angle: compare personal narrative, contemporary documentation and later scene histories.
- Status: draft.

## Entry DE-09 — Martha Cooper Library
- ID: archive.martha-cooper-library
- Type: Research institution / archive
- Short definition: A specialist library at URBAN NATION Museum in Berlin holding publications and archival material on graffiti, street art and urban art, including rare books, fanzines and 1980s/1990s graffiti magazines.
- Context: The institution describes its collection as based on Martha Cooper's donation and permanent loans, with a strong focus on U.S.-tradition style writing and international material. [MC-01]
- Distinguish from: a freely reusable image repository. Reading-room access and catalogue records do not grant image reproduction rights.
- Learning angle: show how books, magazines, fanzines and archives transmit styles and preserve ephemeral culture.
- Related: Martha Cooper, Subway Art, German regional scenes, photographic archive.
- Status: draft.
