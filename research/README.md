# NIGHTSHIFT Research Library

**Purpose:** source-backed knowledge base for THE BLACKBOOK ARCHIVE and the NIGHTSHIFT Piece Generator.

## Editorial method

Each research statement must be traceable to a source. Separate:
- **Documented fact:** directly supported by a cited source.
- **Practitioner account:** oral history or first-person recollection; valuable, but situated and sometimes contested.
- **Scholarly interpretation:** an author's analysis.
- **Design inference:** a proposed translation into game/editor behavior, not a historical fact.

### Evidence labels
- **A — Strong:** primary archive, museum collection, scholarly book/article, or direct oral history with provenance.
- **B — Good:** established cultural institution or specialist publication with named author and references.
- **C — Contextual:** useful secondary overview; corroborate before relying on it for precise claims.
- **D — Unverified:** lead only; do not use as settled fact in the game.

Evidence labels assess the support for a particular claim, not the prestige of a source in general.

## Rights and image handling

A visible online image is not automatically reusable. The visual-reference database records links and provenance; do not copy image files into the game unless rights, license or permission are confirmed. Prefer original commissioned diagrams and illustrations for interactive explanations.

## Taxonomy rule

Always distinguish:
- practice/activity (e.g. writing, tagging, bombing),
- lettering approach/style (e.g. bubble lettering, wildstyle),
- format/surface (e.g. panel, wholecar),
- medium/technique (e.g. aerosol, stencil, paste-up),
- cultural actors (writers, crews, photographers, institutions),
- historical period/place.

Terms vary across eras, boroughs, crews and individual writers. Record variation instead of forcing false universal definitions.
