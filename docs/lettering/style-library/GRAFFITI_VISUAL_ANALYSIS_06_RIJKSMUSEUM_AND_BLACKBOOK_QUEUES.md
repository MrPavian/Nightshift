# Letterform Research Pass 06 — Rijksmuseum and Dutch/US Blackbook Queues

**Date:** 2026-11-19  
**Status:** source and page-level acquisition expanded; no new glyph observations accepted.

## What changed

Created a typed sidecar research queue at `letterform-research-queue.v1.json`. It separates source records, page-level catalogue records and future glyph observations. The dataset currently contains seven source records and six named page/book records.

### Highest-priority new page leads

1. **Rijksmuseum, Blackbook I, page 6** — Boris Tellegen / DELTA, 1986–1992, object RP-T-2021-450-6. The museum catalogue describes three pieces on one pencil-drawn page. This is a promising multi-piece page for repeated-form comparison.
2. **Rijksmuseum, Blackbook I, page 7** — object RP-T-2021-450-7. The catalogue identifies two colour photographs and one design drawing. Keep the drawing and photographs separate until visual correspondence is established.
3. **Dutch Graffiti Library, Eras** — 1990–2020. The collection catalogue explicitly describes tags, throw-ups and pieces. Individual contributors and page-level objects still need to be established.
4. **Dutch Graffiti Library, Colt & Deal** — 1985–1989. A useful period-specific blackbook lead; distinguish sketches from reproduced train/subway documentation.
5. **Full Time Artists Ink Outlines** — monochrome sketchbook works from 1991 onward, made with Rapidograph/inkers, markers and ballpoints. Some are described as blueprints for full-scale spray pieces. This is the strongest acquisition route for clean construction evidence once stable individual page URLs and credits are captured.

A lower-priority Rijksmuseum page 28 record is retained because the catalogue mentions the letters DELTA on a belt buckle, but this is unlikely to support robust glyph comparison.

## Evidence boundary

The source catalogues were inspected, but the individual page images for the new Rijksmuseum and Dutch Graffiti Library leads were not available for reliable direct visual analysis in this pass. They are therefore marked `catalogued_page_not_visually_reviewed`. No alphabet letter, style family, construction sequence or glyph count has been inferred from metadata alone.

The previously reviewed Letterform Archive objects remain in the main manifest. They are not silently promoted to glyph-level observations: editorial mastheads, small train photographs and multi-piece pages remain object-level evidence unless a particular glyph can be isolated and read.

## Rights

All new third-party records are link-only. Rijksmuseum records explicitly identify Boris Tellegen copyright. Full Time Artists states that its content is copyrighted by FTA/original authors and prohibits unauthorized duplication or distribution. Dutch Graffiti Library object-level reuse terms remain to be checked. No image files were copied into NIGHTSHIFT.

## Next inspection order

1. Obtain and inspect the high-resolution Rijksmuseum page 6 and page 7 images.
2. Open individual Full Time Artists ink-outline pages and capture stable page URLs, creator credits and visible words.
3. Inspect Dutch Graffiti Library Eras and Colt & Deal page sequences; record contributor identity per page, not just book owner.
4. For each eligible word, record only clearly legible glyphs and their local relationships. Keep letter skeleton, outline, fill, inline and depth as separate layers.
5. Promote a glyph only after direct review; record the image/page location, confidence and any ambiguity.
