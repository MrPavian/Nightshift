## 2. Visual observations supported by the available records

### NGA-HC-001 — Hand of Doom by Seen
The NGA catalogue identifies four chromogenic prints and records an inscription naming the work as “HaND OF DOOM (by SeeN)”. Its image sheet is approximately 17.78 × 103.25 cm. The available visual preview shows a long train-car composition with prominent illustrated subject matter and lettering integrated into that field.

**Use:** study the separation and hierarchy between name lettering, narrative illustration, background and secondary elements.  
**Do not classify as a pure Bubble specimen:** the available evidence supports a complex piece/composition reference, not a clean isolated bubble alphabet.  
**Do not copy:** the SEEN name, hand motif, letter sequence or overall layout.

### NGA-HC-002 — Stop the Bomb by Lee
The NGA catalogue identifies five chromogenic prints and records the inscription “STOP The BOMb (by Lee)”. Its image sheet is approximately 17.78 × 100.97 cm. The NGA visual description identifies large bold message lettering, surrounded by anti-war phrases and symbols.

**Use:** study word-scale hierarchy, broad letter mass, message readability and interaction between main lettering and secondary text/symbols.  
**Do not classify as Blockbuster by default:** “large and bold” is not enough to prove a specific letterform grammar. The actual glyph anatomy still needs direct inspection.  
**Do not copy:** the message-piece layout, letter sequence or distinctive symbol system.

## 4. Broader collection value

The NGA artist index exposes 29 Henry Chalfant works with titles, dates, accession IDs, print counts and selected visual descriptions. This is a valuable secondary acquisition route beyond the Smithsonian's 24-print finding aid. The list includes named objects such as *Style Wars by Noc* (1981), *Passion by Zephyr* (1981), *I Love Zoo York by Ali* (1981), *Crasher* (1980), *Kel Futura* (1980), *Hand of Doom by Seen* (1980) and *Stop the Bomb by Lee* (1979).

The catalogue descriptions are not substitutes for image inspection. Keep style labels provisional until actual letterforms are visible at adequate resolution.

## 5. Research consequence

This source group reinforces a key NIGHTSHIFT design rule: a graffiti piece is not reducible to its letter family. The same long train-car field can combine lettering, illustration, slogans, tags and graphic devices. Store these as separate composition layers and do not let a piece-level visual label leak into the glyph generator.
