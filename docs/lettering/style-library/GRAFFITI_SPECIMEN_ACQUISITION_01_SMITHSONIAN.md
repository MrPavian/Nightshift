## 1. What has been verified

The Smithsonian catalogue identifies a 24-print series of chromogenic photographic prints by Henry Chalfant, dated 1977–1980. The item-level search results expose individual titles, identifiers, formats/dimensions, dates for many records, and archival GUIDs.

The catalogue also states that access requires an appointment. Reproduction may be requested, but the Archives does not guarantee that copyright restrictions are cleared; other intellectual-property rights may apply. Therefore every record remains **link-only / rights clearance pending**.

## 3. What has deliberately NOT been inferred

No style family has been assigned to these specimens yet. A catalogue title and print dimensions cannot prove Bubble, Throw-up, Simple/Straight, Blockbuster or Wildstyle. The actual image must be viewed before recording:
- glyph anatomy and silhouette;
- whether a full word or only a crop is visible;
- letter connections and word rhythm;
- execution format and rendering treatment;
- candidate style family and confidence.

The manifest therefore marks all 15 as `metadata_verified_image_not_inspected`. This is intentional: catalogue acquisition is complete for these records, but visual annotation is not.

## 4. Priority order for image inspection

1. **Long coupler prints:** SI-CH-001, 002, 006, 008, 009, 010, 013. These formats are promising for whole-word and multi-writer composition analysis.
2. **Single-name / smaller prints:** SI-CH-003, 004, 005, 007, 011, 012, 014, 015. Inspect whether the crop reveals individual letter construction or only a partial word.
3. **Cross-compare repeated letterforms:** where the actual images show shared letters (e.g. A, E, H, M, R, S), compare across different specimens rather than declaring one canonical glyph.

## 5. Access and rights

- Public catalogue metadata may be cited and linked.
- Physical collection use requires an appointment with the Anacostia Community Museum Archives.
- Reproduction is subject to archive procedures, fees and separate copyright / intellectual-property clearance.
- Do not download, embed or redistribute image files on the assumption that Smithsonian catalogue access means open licensing.
- Do not reproduce a writer's name, signature, exact letter sequence or full composition as a NIGHTSHIFT template.
