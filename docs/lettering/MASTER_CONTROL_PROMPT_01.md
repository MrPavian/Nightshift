# NIGHTSHIFT – LETTER CONSTRUCTION ACADEMY
## MASTER CONTROL PROMPT 01 – LETTER STUDY

**Version:** 1.1  
**Reference:** A  
**Scope:** Uppercase A–Z, lowercase a–z, numerals 0–9

## 1. Purpose

**The destination is original graffiti lettering.** Typography and calligraphy are foundations for understanding construction, rhythm, negative space and tool behaviour; they are not the end product. Graffiti must be a central learning objective, not a small decorative panel added to a typography worksheet.

Each study must balance letter construction, anatomy, calligraphy/handstyle/ductus, graffiti-specific letterform design, composition and practical development of original lettering. Validate uppercase A as a complete graffiti-led teaching sheet before scaling.

## 2. Fixed visual language

- DIN A4 landscape, 1.414:1; light neutral paper and subtle grid.
- Main construction black; construction lines violet; guides yellow.
- German labels; established graffiti terms may remain in English and should be explained where useful.
- Precise editorial blackbook / lettering-workshop aesthetic.
- Diagrams large, legible and visually distinct.
- Graffiti examples must be substantial and clearly more than typed font glyphs with an outline.

## 3. Mandatory nine-part structure

01 — **Grundform und Skelett:** neutral base glyph and underlying skeleton, separate from graffiti styling.

02 — **Buchstabenanatomie und Negativform:** label only components that actually occur; show counters, gaps and negative spaces as active design areas.

03 — **Proportionen und Spannung:** height, width, baseline, component ratios, crossbar position and tension between angles, widths and spaces. No universal ratio assumptions.

04 — **Konstruktion zum Letter:** coherent sequence from skeleton to stroke body, structural components, contour and filled basic letter. Each step visibly builds on the previous one; no repeated near-identical silhouettes.

05 — **Graffiti-Letterform-Variationen:** at least five meaningfully different original graffiti-oriented constructions. Vary structure, width, angle, weight, terminals, joins, overlap or rhythm. These must be actual letterform decisions, not merely colour/effect changes.

06 — **Style-Entscheidungen:** compare form tension, rhythm, connections, overlap, spacing, negative space and readability as design dimensions, not rigid style categories.

07 — **Kalligrafie, Handstyle und Duktus:** connect calligraphic exercises and tool behaviour to handstyle development. Distinguish visible shape from movement and tool behaviour. Static outlines do not prove stroke order, speed, pressure or tool movement. A calligraphic exercise is not automatically a graffiti handstyle.

08 — **Graffiti-Anwendung:** at least three substantial, original, clearly differentiated explorations, e.g. handstyle-led, block/throw-up-oriented and piece-oriented. These are parallel directions, not a universal Tag → Throw-up → Piece progression. Structural choices matter; outline, fill, inline, shadow and highlights are secondary and must support the letter.

09 — **Graffiti-Lernkontrolle:** three to five practical exercises; at least one produces a finished original graffiti-letter interpretation.

## 4. Technical and editorial rules

- Keep anatomy accurate and construction logically explainable.
- Distinguish skeleton, stroke body, contour, fill and presentation effects.
- Treat counters and negative spaces as active design elements.
- Never force every letter into the same construction logic.
- No unsupported historical claims; do not infer ductus from static images.
- Treat calligraphy, typography, handstyle and graffiti lettering as related but distinct.
- Never present Tag → Throw-up → Piece as a universal ladder.
- Do not mistake plain typed fonts for meaningful graffiti-letter construction.
- Never copy identifiable writers, signatures, crews or existing pieces. All demonstrations are original fictional designs.
- Effects are secondary to strong letter construction.

## 5. Consistency and quality control

Keep page structure, section order, colour conventions, scale and German terminology consistent. Adapt anatomy to each character. Before delivery verify: all nine sections; correct neutral glyph; coherent construction; structurally distinct graffiti variants; substantial graffiti application; handstyle/ductus distinction; legible labels; original designs; exercises leading to original graffiti lettering.

**Reject and revise any sheet where graffiti appears only as a minor final add-on.**

## 6. Production instruction

Create one complete study for the requested character only. Current reference: **A**. No alphabet overview for an individual-letter request.

**Graffiti lettering is the destination. Construction is the foundation. Calligraphy is one influence—not the finish line.**
